# Track weighted-average cost in TWD, not in the transaction's original currency

Every transaction converts to TWD using its own trade-date FX rate before being folded into a symbol's running weighted-average cost — the average itself lives in TWD, not in USD for US-market holdings. This follows directly from reporting everything as one TWD total (see `CONTEXT.md`), but it has a consequence worth calling out: FX movement between a US-market buy and a later sell (or the current date, for unrealized P&L) is implicitly part of the reported P&L. A US stock that is flat in USD terms but where USD has strengthened against TWD since purchase will show a paper gain, and vice versa — the system does not separate "stock P&L" from "currency P&L" as two figures. This was accepted as the simpler, single-number model rather than building a second reporting dimension for currency effects; a future reader seeing a US position gain or lose money independent of any USD price change should look here first, not assume a bug.
